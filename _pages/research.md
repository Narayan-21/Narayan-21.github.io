<p>
  <a href="http://eprints.iiserkol.ac.in/1379/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Quantitative analysis of metal distribution along the Ganga (Hooghly) estuary: Towards establishing the inter-relation between surface water, groundwater and estuarine water</a><br>
  Narayan Jangid · Master's thesis, IISER Kolkata, 2022 · Supervisor: Dr. Gopala Krishna Darbha
</p>

- Quantified heavy-metal contamination across groundwater, surface water, soil and sediment of the Hooghly estuary, and compared it against WHO drinking-water and soil standards.
- Found arsenic in surface water and groundwater, and manganese in groundwater, above WHO limits (0.01 mg/L As, 0.05 mg/L Mn).
- Assessed sediment toxicity and ecological risk with the geoaccumulation index, potential ecological risk index and toxic risk index.
- Used correlation analysis to study how surface-water and groundwater contamination influence each other.
